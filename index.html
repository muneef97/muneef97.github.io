Core Four: reading rock from drill-core mineral scans

GSWA HyLogger Hackathon 2026 · Geological Survey of Western Australia · team of 4 · I was data engineer and team captain

GSWA's HyLogger scanners record the minerals in drill core, metre by metre. We built an open, reproducible Python pipeline and two interactive apps that turn those scans into rock types, rock boundaries, chemistry estimates and data-quality checks, across 65 drill holes from 40 companies.

  • Built the 20-script pipeline that runs end to end from one command and stops at the first error
  • Joined mineral logs, assays and geology logs to every metre, and wrote the data-quality rules that set aside about 2,900 doubtful assay values
  • Read 76,160 raw spectra with our own open code, matching the commercial software's white-mica result (r = 0.90)
  • Validated every result on holes, or whole companies, the models never saw

Churn Busters: predicting health insurance cancellations

WA Data Science Innovation Hub Hackathon 2026 · HBF Challenge 1 · team of 3 · presented at HBF head office, Oct 2026

HBF asked which policies are about to cancel and what to do about each one. We built a LightGBM pipeline that scores every active policy for its 3-month cancellation risk, explains the warning signs for each type of cancellation, and picks the most cost-effective way to keep each member.

  • Built the data foundation: leak-safe labels over about 11.3 million policy-months, and a date-ordered train/test split with gap months
  • Engineered features and trained LightGBM models, with SHAP and shuffle tests to explain what drives each cancellation type
  • Turned risk scores into a retention plan for all 538,738 active policies, worth about $1.79M a month
Catching a leak: an early version scored 97.6%. We traced it to three fields that are filled in while a cancellation is processed, removed them, and reported the honest result instead.
